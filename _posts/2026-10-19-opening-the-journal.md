This site records an experiment in trading options with an AI system as implementation partner. The aim is simple: document what we build, report on it regularly, and measure whether it works, including when it does not.

Entries will appear in three places. The Journal covers design decisions and lessons. Reports review the desk over a period. Results holds the measured outcomes, with the method stated up front so the figures can be judged on their merits.
