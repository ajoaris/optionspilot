Options Pilot is written by Alain Joaris, formerly Head of Finance, Contracts and Audit in an EU Delegation, now applying the same habit of scrutiny to an AI-assisted options trading desk.

The desk sells defined-risk options premium (credit spreads and iron condors), following the mechanical approach popularised by Tastytrade: entry around 45 days to expiration, winners managed at 50% of maximum profit, exit by 21 days to expiration. The software screens candidates, estimates probabilities and proposes trades. The rules are mine; the AI is the implementation partner.

## Notice

This is a personal research journal. Nothing here is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Options trading involves substantial risk of loss.
