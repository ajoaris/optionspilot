Results are reported as percentages of capital at risk. Account sizes are not published.

## Method

- **Return on capital at risk**: profit or loss of each closed trade divided by its maximum possible loss.
- **Win rate**: share of closed trades that ended in profit.
- **Average days held**: from entry to close.
- **Largest drawdown**: the deepest peak-to-trough decline over the period.

Shadow trades, which the system proposes but does not execute, are reported separately from live trades.
